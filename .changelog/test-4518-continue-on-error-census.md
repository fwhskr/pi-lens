- **The continue-on-error census now covers the whole producer tree (refs #4518)** — every `continue-on-error` site in `.github/workflows/*.{yml,yaml}` and in a composite `.github/actions/**/action.yml|action.yaml` is registered in `tests/fixtures/nightly-floors/continue-on-error-sites.json` as floored (naming the gating step, whose floor must sit in the argument list of a named measuring invocation on a top-level line of its `run:`), no-floor (the reader that makes an empty result visible), or governed-by (the issue and the live `it`/`test` declarations of the tests that own it). The test derives the population from the parsed files, and both of its predicates are allowlists, so a shape neither lists refuses: an unregistered site, a producer file it cannot classify, a key outside a job or step, floor evidence that is not a measuring invocation's argument (a comment, a quoted command, a heredoc body, a printed command, a function or block body, a subshell, a chained command, or a line after an `exit`), and a floor step whose `if:` is anything but absent or a conjunction of `always()`, `success()`, `!cancelled()` and one `github.event_name` or `matrix.<name>` comparison the workflow's own `on:` block or that job's `strategy.matrix` lists, of a job whose `if:` the document cannot prove never true. A `\`-continued or `&&`-continued line is one command, so a floor must start the joined command, and an `exit` ends the script only in command position. The Swift crash-watch step also prints `evaluated=<0|1>` to its log, so a night read from the job log alone states whether swift was evaluated.
